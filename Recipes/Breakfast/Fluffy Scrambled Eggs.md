# Fluffy Scrambled Eggs

**Servings:** Variable; scale by number of medium eggs  
**Prep time:** 10–15 minutes resting time  
**Cook time:** Not timed; cook gently until just set

## Ingredients

### Baseline — for every 2 medium eggs

- 2 medium eggs
- 2 tsp milk
- Small pinch of salt, to taste
- 1/2–1 tsp butter
- 3–5 tbsp shredded cheese (optional)

### Quick Scaling Reference

| Medium eggs | Milk | Butter | Shredded cheese (optional) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 2 tsp | 1/2–1 tsp | 3–5 tbsp |
| 6 | 2 tbsp | 1 1/2–3 tsp | 9–15 tbsp |
| 10 | 3 tbsp + 1 tsp | 2 1/2–5 tsp | 15–25 tbsp |
| 12 | 1/4 cup | 1–2 tbsp | 18–30 tbsp |

Salt is to taste rather than multiplied mechanically. Larger batches may require extra cooking time or more than one skillet batch.

## Directions

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
2. Add milk and a small pinch of salt.
3. Whisk well until fully combined.
4. Let the egg mixture rest on the counter for 10–15 minutes. **Do not add cheese during the rest.**
5. Heat a skillet around setting `3` on the electric coil stove (`LO`, `1–9`, `HI`).
6. Melt the butter gently without browning.
7. Pour in the eggs.
8. Stir slowly and fold gently with a spatula as the eggs set.
9. **If using cheese:** add it when the eggs are approximately halfway set but still glossy. Fold it in gently.
10. Continue cooking at low to medium-low heat until just set.
11. Remove from heat while the eggs are still slightly soft and glossy. Carryover heat will finish them.

## Notes

- Stove reference: `LO`, `1–9`, `HI`; start around `3`, generally within `2.5–4`. For fluffier eggs, favor `2.5–3`.
- If the eggs sizzle hard or brown, reduce the heat below `3`.
- For fluffier eggs, use the lower end of the cheese range; for richer eggs, use more.
- Too much milk can make eggs watery; avoid cooking until dry.
