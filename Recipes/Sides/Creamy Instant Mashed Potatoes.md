# Creamy Instant Mashed Potatoes

**Servings:** 5 (approximately 1/2 cup each)  
**Prep time:** Not established  
**Cook time:** Time to boil water, plus 1 minute resting

## Ingredients

- 1 2/3 cups water
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1/4–3/8 tsp salt, to taste
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 2–3 oz cream cheese
- 1 2/3 cups instant mashed potato flakes
- Small pinch black pepper (optional)
- Tiny pinch garlic powder (optional)

## Directions

1. Combine water, butter, and salt in a saucepan.
2. Heat over medium-high, about `6–7` on the electric coil stove, until the water reaches a boil.
3. Remove saucepan from the heat.
4. Stir in milk, sour cream, and cream cheese until the cream cheese is mostly melted and the mixture reasonably smooth.
5. Gradually stir in potato flakes using a fork until evenly incorporated.
6. Rest for **1 minute**.
7. Stir gently again to reach the desired consistency.
8. Season with black pepper or garlic powder if desired.

## Notes

### Cooking Tips

- Avoid overmixing, which can make potatoes pasty. Use a fork, not aggressive whipping.
- Add flakes gradually. For thicker potatoes, add a little more; for thinner, add a splash of milk.
- Remove from heat before adding dairy and flakes.

### Original Package Proportions for 5 Servings

- 1 2/3 cups water
- 2 1/2 tbsp butter or margarine
- Approximately 3/8 tsp salt
- 5/8 cup milk
- 1 2/3 cups instant mashed potato flakes
