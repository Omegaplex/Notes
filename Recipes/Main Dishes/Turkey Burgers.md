# Turkey Burgers

**Servings:** 10 patties per 3-lb batch  
**Prep time:** Approximately 15 minutes, plus 20–30 minutes chilling  
**Cook time:** Approximately 10–13 minutes per skillet batch

## Ingredients

### Turkey Burgers

- 3 lb ground turkey (85% lean)
- 3 eggs
- 3/4 cup plain breadcrumbs, plus additional if needed
- 1–2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 3 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 3/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 1/2 tsp garlic powder (optional)
- 1–2 tsp cooking oil for the skillet

### Dijon Variation

- 2 tsp Dijon mustard per 3 lb turkey

## Directions

1. Place turkey in a large mixing bowl.
2. Add eggs, breadcrumbs, mayonnaise, Worcestershire, salt, black pepper, and optional garlic powder.
3. For the Dijon version, add 2 tsp Dijon mustard.
4. Mix gently until combined. Mixture will be soft and sticky.
5. If excessively wet and unable to hold shape, add a little more breadcrumbs.
6. Cover and refrigerate 20–30 minutes.
7. Divide into 10 portions and form patties about 3/4 inch thick; make a shallow indentation in each center.
8. Heat a nonstick skillet to medium, starting around `5–6` on the electric coil stove (`LO`, `1–9`, `HI`).
9. Add 1–2 tsp oil and coat pan.
10. Add patties without crowding. Cook approximately 5–6 minutes on first side, until browned below and turning opaque partway up.
11. Flip carefully and cook 5–7 minutes more.
12. For thick patties, lower heat and loosely cover for the last 3–4 minutes if needed.
13. Check the thickest center with a thermometer. Cook to **165°F**.
14. Rest 3–5 minutes before serving.

## Notes

### Cooking

- Recommendation is **3/4 inch** for more even cooking.
- Avoid repeated flipping and check **165°F** internally rather than using time alone.
