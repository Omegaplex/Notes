# Buttery Skillet Burgers

**Servings:** 12 patties  
**Prep time:** Approximately 15 minutes, plus optional refrigeration  
**Cook time:** Approximately 8–12 minutes per batch; verify internal temperature

## Ingredients

- 4 lb 80/20 ground beef
- 1 tsp butter per skillet batch
- 1 tsp garlic powder (optional)
- Black pepper, to taste
- Table salt, to taste
- Velveeta 2% cheese, 1 slice per burger (optional)

## Directions

1. Divide ground beef into 12 equal portions, approximately 5 1/3 oz each.
2. Gently form into patties slightly wider than the hamburger buns. Do not pack or overwork the meat.
3. Press a shallow indentation into the center of each patty to reduce swelling.
4. If preparing ahead, cover and refrigerate until ready to cook.
5. Preheat a nonstick skillet over medium heat, starting at `4` on the electric coil stove (`LO`, `1–9`, `HI`).
6. Just before cooking, lightly season one side with table salt, black pepper, and a little garlic powder if desired.
7. Place seasoned side down in the dry skillet, then season the exposed side.
8. Cook approximately 4–5 minutes on the first side to develop a crust.
9. Flip and cook approximately 4–5 minutes more, adjusting heat if the exterior browns too quickly.
10. Near the end of cooking, add **1 tsp butter** for that skillet batch. When melted, spoon over patties.
11. Insert a meat thermometer horizontally into the thickest patty and continue cooking until the center reaches **160°F**.
12. If using Velveeta 2%, add one slice during the last 30–60 seconds; cover briefly to melt if needed.
13. Remove from skillet and serve.

## Notes

### Cooking Improvements

- Original stove setting `5–6` browned the outside too quickly; start around `4`.
- Thin patties if needed for more even cooking. Original exact thickness was not recorded.
- Do not press patties down with a spatula.
- Cooking time is approximate; verify **160°F** in the center, not appearance alone.
