# Pepperoni, Sausage & Cheese Calzones

**Servings:** 4 medium calzones  
**Prep time:** 25–30 minutes, plus 60–90 minutes dough resting  
**Cook time:** Approximately 40 minutes total (sausage and two baking batches)

## Ingredients

### Calzones

- 2 packages (16 oz each) Kroger refrigerated pizza dough
- 1 lb mild Italian sausage
- 4 cups low-moisture, part-skim shredded mozzarella cheese
- Diced pepperoni, up to approximately 5 oz (use to taste)
- Pizza sauce, for dipping
- All-purpose flour, for stretching dough
- Olive oil, for brushing dough and oiling pan

### Garlic Butter Finish

- 3 tbsp salted butter
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp Italian seasoning

## Directions

1. **Rest the dough.** Divide the refrigerated pizza dough into four equal portions. Place on parchment paper, lightly coat with olive oil, and cover loosely with lightly oiled plastic wrap. Rest at room temperature (approximately 70°F) for 60–90 minutes.

2. **Cook the sausage.** Brown the sausage in a skillet over medium heat (5–6 on an electric coil stove), breaking it into small crumbles. Cook thoroughly to 160°F, approximately 8–10 minutes. Drain the grease and allow to cool slightly.

3. **Preheat the oven.** Set the oven to 450°F with a rack in the lower-middle position. Preheat for at least 20 minutes. Lightly oil an 18-inch perforated pizza pan.

4. **Stretch the dough.** Lightly flour the work surface. Flatten each dough portion with your fingertips and gently stretch into a circle approximately 9–10 inches across. Aim for ⅛–¼ inch thickness, leaving the edges slightly thicker. If the dough springs back, cover and rest another 10 minutes before continuing.

5. **Add the filling.** On one half of each dough circle, leaving a 1-inch border, layer:
   - Approximately ½ cup mozzarella
   - ¼ lb cooked sausage
   - Diced pepperoni, divided to taste
   - Remaining ½ cup mozzarella

6. **Fold and seal.** Fold the empty half over the filling. Press the edges together, fold the seam over itself, and crimp firmly. Cut two small steam vents into the top.

7. **Prepare for baking.** Place two calzones directly on the lightly oiled perforated pizza pan, leaving space between them. Lightly brush the tops with olive oil. Do not use parchment during baking.

8. **Bake.** Bake at 450°F for **15 minutes**, until the tops are golden brown and the bottoms are crisp and fully cooked. Repeat with the remaining two calzones.

9. **Prepare the garlic butter.** Melt the salted butter and stir in the garlic powder and Italian seasoning.

10. **Finish.** Immediately after baking, lightly brush the calzones with garlic butter, concentrating on the raised crust edges.

11. **Rest and serve.** Transfer to a wire rack and rest for approximately 5 minutes. Serve with warm pizza sauce for dipping.

## Notes

- **Equipment:** An 18-inch perforated pizza pan produces a crispy crust by allowing hot air to circulate beneath the dough.
- **Baking:** 450°F for 15 minutes produced good crust texture and flavor in the first batch.
- **Dough resting:** The initial 45-minute rest left the dough difficult to keep stretched. The recommended 60–90-minute rest is a suggested improvement and has not yet been tested.
- **Pepperoni:** The initial batch had some pepperoni left over. Adjust the quantity to preference.
- **Sauce:** Keep pizza sauce outside the calzones to prevent excess moisture from softening the crust.
- **Garlic butter:** Brush on lightly after baking to preserve crispness while adding buttery flavor.
- **Filling:** The full pound of sausage and all 4 cups of mozzarella were used successfully.
