# Restaurant-Style Grilled Chicken Quesadillas with Velveeta Queso Blanco Dip

**Servings:** 8 large folded quesadillas  
**Prep time:** 30 minutes minimum seasoning time; about 1 hour preferred  
**Cook time:** About 45–60 minutes total, depending on batches

## Ingredients

### Chicken

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Boneless skinless chicken thighs | 3.25 lb |
| Oil | 3 tbsp |
| Salt | 1 3/4 tsp, or 2 tsp for stronger flavor |
| Garlic powder | 1 3/4 tsp |
| Onion powder | 1 3/4 tsp |
| Chili powder | 1 3/4 tsp |
| Paprika | 1 3/4 tsp |
| Cumin | 3/4 tsp |
| Black pepper | 3/4 tsp |
| Sugar | Scant 1/2 tsp |
| Bottled lime juice | 1–1 1/2 tbsp, added after cooking |

### Quesadillas

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Large white flour burrito-size tortillas | 8 |
| Shredded Monterey Jack cheese | 4–6 cups |
| Butter or cooking spray | Light coating for tortillas |

### Velveeta Queso Blanco Dip

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Velveeta Queso Blanco | 16 oz |
| Evaporated milk | Start with 1/2 cup; add more as needed |
| Butter | 1 tbsp |
| Garlic powder | 1/4 tsp |
| Onion powder | 1/4 tsp |
| Cumin | Tiny pinch |
| Chili powder | Tiny pinch, optional |
| Salt | Only if needed after tasting |

## Directions

### 1. Prep the Chicken

1. Open pack and separate the chicken thighs. **Leave mostly whole**, opening folded or very thick pieces flat. Trim only large loose fat or stringy bits.
2. Combine oil, salt, garlic powder, onion powder, chili powder, paprika, cumin, black pepper, and sugar in a large zip bag or bowl.
3. Add chicken and massage to coat evenly. **Do not add lime juice yet.**
4. Spread chicken flat in the bag rather than piling it up, and refrigerate to season: **20–30 minutes** is sufficient; **1 hour** better; **2 hours** very good. Longer is fine if lime was omitted.
5. Take chicken out of refrigerator about **10–15 minutes** before cooking while preheating the Griddler.

### 2. Cook the Chicken

1. Preheat **Cuisinart Griddler GR-4NP1 to 375°F**, preferably with flat plates. Grill plates work too.
2. Lift out chicken with tongs, letting excess oily marinade drip off. Do not pour marinade onto Griddler.
3. Lay thighs in a single layer in batches. Close lid gently.
4. Cook until thickest pieces reach **165°F**, approximately **6–8 minutes** for thin thighs and **9–12 minutes** for thicker ones.
5. If seasoning goes black rather than deep reddish-brown, lower heat to **350°F**.
6. Rest on a plate or board for **5–10 minutes**.

### 3. Make the Queso

1. While chicken rests, put **1 tbsp butter**, **1/2 cup evaporated milk**, and **16 oz cubed Velveeta Queso Blanco** in a saucepan over low heat.
2. Stir slowly until smooth; add more evaporated milk a splash at a time for dipping consistency.
3. Season with garlic powder, onion powder, tiny pinch of cumin, and optional chili powder.
4. Taste before adding salt. **Do not boil.** Keep warm on low.

### 4. Finish the Chicken

1. Chop rested chicken into small bite-size pieces.
2. Toss with **1–1 1/2 tbsp bottled lime juice** and a spoonful of resting juices, if available.
3. Taste and add a small pinch of salt if flat. Avoid leaving the filling in pooled liquid; use tongs or slotted spoon when filling tortillas.

### 5. Assemble

1. Reduce Griddler to **350°F**, using flat plates.
2. For each white flour burrito-size tortilla, spread Monterey Jack on one half, add chopped chicken, then more cheese, and fold.
3. Use around **1/2 cup Monterey Jack total per quesadilla**, or **2/3 cup** for extra cheese. Do not overfill.
4. Reserve Velveeta queso **mostly for dipping**; excessive queso inside makes a soggy tortilla.

### 6. Cook the Quesadillas

1. Lightly coat outsides with cooking spray or softened butter, around **1/2 tsp per side** if using butter.
2. Place on Griddler and close lid gently without hard pressure.
3. Cook **3–5 minutes**, checking at 3, until tortilla is golden, cheese melted, and surface lightly crisp.
4. Reduce to **325°F** if the butter smokes or tortilla browns before cheese melts.

### 7. Hold for Serving

1. If making all eight ahead, preheat oven to **200°F**.
2. Set a wire rack over a sheet pan, keeping cooked quesadillas **uncovered and unstacked** on rack in oven.
3. Cut just before serving. Stacking or placing directly on a tray traps steam and softens tortillas.

## Notes

### Preferred Ingredients

- **Heritage Farm** boneless skinless chicken thighs (3.25-lb pack).
- **Kroger** shredded Monterey Jack, regular large white flour burrito-size tortillas, **Velveeta Queso Blanco** dip, and bottled lime juice.

### Recipe Choices

- Restaurant-style flavor; **no vegetables**, white cheese.
- Mission Carb Balance Whole Wheat tortillas were discussed but rejected in favor of white flour tortillas.
- Original cheese idea was Monterey Jack or Oaxaca plus white American; white American was hard to find. Practical recipe uses Monterey Jack in the quesadillas and Velveeta Queso Blanco for dipping.
- Smoked sharp white cheddar was considered and rejected as the main queso cheese (too smoky/sharp; could get grainy/oily).
- Cook chicken whole, then rest and chop for juiciness; adding lime after cooking helps preserve texture.
- Scant sugar helps browning but can scorch; reduce 375°F to 350°F if blackening.
