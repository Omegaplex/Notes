# Turkey Meatloaf

**Servings:** 18 slices  
**Prep time:** Approximately 15–20 minutes  
**Cook time:** Approximately 60–75 minutes at 350°F, plus 10 minutes resting

## Ingredients

### Meatloaf

- 3 lb ground turkey (85% lean)
- 3 eggs
- 1–1 1/4 cups plain breadcrumbs
- 3 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp ketchup
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard (optional)
- 2–3 tbsp milk
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 3/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- Canola oil cooking spray for dish

### Ketchup Glaze

- 1/4 cup ketchup
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to **350°F**.
2. Put ground turkey in a large bowl.
3. Add eggs, breadcrumbs, Worcestershire sauce, ketchup, optional Dijon, milk, salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
4. Mix gently until combined; do not overmix.
5. Lightly grease the bottom and lower sides of a **3-quart rectangular Pyrex baking dish** with canola oil cooking spray.
6. Shape mixture into a loaf centered in the dish, approximately **2–2 1/2 inches high**, leaving space around it for juices.
7. Bake uncovered at **350°F**.
8. Mix glaze ingredients in a small bowl.
9. After approximately **40–45 minutes**, spread glaze over meatloaf.
10. Continue baking until the center reaches **165°F**, roughly **60–75 minutes total**, depending on thickness.
11. Rest **10 minutes** before slicing.
12. Cut into **18 slices** and serve.

## Notes

### Cooking Tips

- Do not pack too tightly or spread the mixture across the whole dish.
- Keep space for rendered fat and juices. Cooking time depends on loaf thickness.
- Verify internal temperature **165°F**; rest 10 minutes before cutting.
