# Church Research Backlog

These are subjects the user wants available for future deep-research conversations. Do not begin a study solely because it appears here. Suggest a topic when the user asks for something substantial to explore, or research it when requested.

## Every believer as a saint

Study the biblical use of *saint* or *holy one*, how it relates to the user's Pentecostal faith, and how Catholic, Orthodox, and other Protestant traditions understand sainthood. Distinguish biblical vocabulary from later recognition and canonization practices.

## Civil, ceremonial, and moral divisions of Old Testament law

Study whether the common three-part division is textually explicit, historically developed, or a useful later framework. Examine its validity, its relationship to the Old and New Testaments, and how it should or should not shape modern Christian practice.
