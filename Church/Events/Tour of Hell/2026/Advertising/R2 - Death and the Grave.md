# R2 — Death and the Grave

Read and follow the shared Tour of Hell Facebook advertising standard in:

Church/Events/Tour of Hell/2026/Advertising/Facebook hype ads.md

Apply that file’s required format, composition, typography hierarchy, hand-drawn concept-art treatment, restrained theatrical scale, biblical tone, and updated TOUROFHELL.CC footer treatment.

Then use the following room-specific direction for Room 2 — Death and the Grave.

SCENE BASIS

Base the image on the current Room 2 script and its biblical references:

- Revelation 6:8
- Psalm 116:3
- Hebrews 9:27

The scene represents Death and the Grave using biblical imagery.

The mood should be solemn, prophetic, restrained, and spiritually weighty — not creepy, monstrous, or designed merely to frighten.

The image should feel like a symbolic biblical tableau taking place inside a darkened interior room filled with mist, with two cloaked figures representing Death and the Grave.

FIGURES

Show two human-like figures standing on opposite sides of the scene.

One represents Death.
One represents the Grave.

Both figures wear simple, dark, full-length cloaks with deep hoods.

Their faces must be completely concealed in shadow.

Do not show:

- eyes
- skin
- facial features
- expressive masks
- skulls
- visible faces of any kind

Each figure holds one plain pillar candle, approximately 3x6 or 3x7 inches.

The candlelight should softly illuminate:

- their hands
- folds of the cloaks
- drifting mist around them

Their posture should be still, solemn, and ceremonial.

They should not lunge, reach, crouch, threaten, or behave aggressively.

They are symbolic personifications of biblical realities, not monsters.

Do not include:

- scythes
- skeletons
- skull imagery
- demons
- weapons
- grotesque features
- fantasy armor
- ghost effects

ENVIRONMENT

The setting is a simple blacked-out interior room.

Smoke and mist fill the air, especially low to the floor.

A very faint orange floor-level glow follows the perimeter of the room, providing subtle separation between the floor and walls.

The orange glow should feel like low theatrical lighting, not flames or lava.

The room should remain mostly dark, with the two candles providing the strongest visible light.

Keep the background simple and restrained.

Do not include:

- cemetery
- tombstones
- gothic castle
- fantasy landscape

BIBLICAL TONE

The scene should visually suggest:

“And I looked, and behold, a pale horse: and his name that sat on him was Death…”
— Revelation 6:8

and especially:

“It is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment.”
— Hebrews 9:27

Do not depict the pale horse literally.

Instead, let the two figures act as symbolic representations of Death and the Grave — realities that eventually confront every person.

The overall message should be inevitability, mortality, and judgment — not fear for fear’s sake.

COMPOSITION

UPPER THIRD

Keep the background very dark and uncluttered.

Use large, bold, condensed white text.

Render the main headline exactly as:

IT IS APPOINTED
UNTO MEN
ONCE TO DIE...

Important: use exactly three periods after DIE, as an ellipsis typed with three separate periods. Do not use two periods. Do not omit the ellipsis.

Directly beneath the main headline, add the scripture reference:

HEBREWS 9:27

The scripture reference should be much smaller than the headline.

Style it like a subtle supporting citation:

- clean
- restrained
- widely spaced letters if appropriate
- white or muted warm ivory
- centered beneath the headline or aligned subtly toward its lower right
- clearly readable, but secondary to the main text

The overall hierarchy should resemble a serious promotional poster: the verse wording dominates, while HEBREWS 9:27 quietly identifies its biblical source.

MIDDLE THIRD

Place one cloaked figure on the left and the other on the right.

Both faces are completely hidden inside dark hoods.

Each holds a single pillar candle.

Leave open darkness between them, filled with mist.

Their positioning should suggest that Death and the Grave are drawing near, but in a quiet, solemn, unavoidable way.

LOWER THIRD

Fade the scene darker toward the bottom.

Use:

TOUROFHELL.CC

Render the website in the shared campaign footer style:

- large elegant distressed serif lettering
- warm ivory / cream text
- subtle orange glow or illumination
- centered on a dark lower background
- framed by thin horizontal fire-like glowing lines above and below
- restrained ember/fire accents rather than large flames
- dramatic and refined
- highly readable on a phone

STYLE

Hand-drawn cinematic concept art.
Dark theatrical key art.
Polished production-concept illustration.
Visible linework and painterly shading.
Biblical allegory.
Solemn and prophetic.
Minimalist theatrical staging.
Quiet tension.
Strong contrast between candlelight and darkness.
Atmospheric mist.
Clearly illustrated rather than photorealistic.
No exaggerated horror imagery.

The finished image should feel like a serious hand-drawn promotional concept painting inspired by Hebrews 9:27 and Revelation 6:8, not a realistic haunted-house photograph.
