## Event identity

- **Event:** Tour of Hell 2026
- **Venue:** Main Street Church of God, Russell Springs, Kentucky
- The event is an immersive theatrical walk-through using live actors, dramatic scenes, lighting, sound, and effects to present a serious biblical message about choices, consequences, judgment, and eternity separated from God.
- The presentation should remain spiritually serious rather than becoming generic horror, gore, or fantasy spectacle.
- Scenes are dramatic/theatrical representations. Visual details should not be presented as claims that every depicted element is a literal description of the afterlife.

## Public dates and hours

Advertised public dates:

- Sunday, October 4, 2026 — 3:00–8:00 PM
- Sunday, October 18, 2026 — 3:00–8:00 PM
- Sunday, October 25, 2026 — 3:00–8:00 PM

Additional private or special reservations may exist on other dates. Do not add those to public advertising unless explicitly approved.

## Tour timing and group flow

- Normal tour cycles are **12 minutes apart**.
- Maximum normal cycle size is **8 guests**.
- Groups do **not overlap** in the tour.
- Each group moves as a single cycle through the route.
- Group/color assignments are used operationally so staff can identify groups.
- Armband quantities should follow the normal 8-person cycle size.

## Guest arrival and registration rules

- Preregistration guarantees the guest's reserved tour date/time.
- Walk-ins are welcome, but are admitted first come, first served after preregistered guests.
- Normal guests should arrive about **10 minutes early**.
- Groups over 20 should arrive about **30 minutes early**.
- Parties should arrive together whenever possible.
- Individual/family registrations are normally **1–7 people**.
- Organized groups are **8 or more people**.
- Capacity planning is based on the 8-person cycle limit.

## Audience and guest safety

- The intended audience is **age 12 and older / middle-school age and older** because of the intensity of the experience.
- Younger children should not be brought through the tour.
- Physical signed waivers are required.
- Minors require a parent/legal-guardian-signed waiver and appropriate adult accompaniment.
- Guest-facing materials should accurately warn about relevant effects when applicable, including:
  - darkness
  - stairs or uneven walking surfaces
  - confined spaces
  - smoke/fog
  - flashing lights
  - loud sounds
  - sudden scares/startle effects
  - slips/falls
  - claustrophobic or sensory effects
- Safety-critical electrical work, rigging, mounting, stairs, platforms, curtains, mirrors, props, and walking surfaces are real event infrastructure. Use secure/rated methods and do not treat safety-critical construction as disposable decoration.

## Script and theology conventions

- Scenes are **thematic, not chronological**.
- The tour should stay rooted in the intended biblical message rather than becoming a generic haunted-house experience.
- A newer confirmed script/theological decision takes priority over wording in an older brochure or flyer.
- Legacy promotional material should be revised to match the final script, not used to force the current script back to older wording.
- The guide ultimately brings the group to the sanctuary/front area before the altar invitation.
- Room-specific theology, dialogue, blocking, and verse choices belong in the individual room/script notes.

## Staffing conventions

- **Outer Darkness should be staffed only by adults.**
- Extra cast may be used in flexible/tentative roles when available; these should be identified as optional rather than required core staffing.
- Cast/staff documents should make it easy to see:
  - role
  - room/location
  - required vs optional staffing
  - age restrictions where applicable
  - group/cycle responsibilities
- Operational staffing should preserve the no-overlap group flow.

## Construction and preparation deadline

- All construction that can be completed **outside the church** should be finished by the weekend of **September 26, 2026**.
- Favor modular/removable construction where practical so pieces can be transported, installed, removed, and stored without rebuilding them from scratch.
- Keep permanent building damage to a minimum when temporary mounting methods will work safely.

## Publicity and copy standards

- Public descriptions should be clear, direct, and understandable when spoken aloud.
- Avoid unnecessarily complicated wording in radio copy.
- The public call to action should emphasize preregistration at **TourOfHell.cc** to guarantee a tour date/time while noting that walk-ins are welcome subject to preregistered guests.
- Public copy may describe the event as an immersive theatrical walk-through using live actors and dramatic scenes to present a serious biblical message about choices, consequences, and eternity separated from God.
- Promotional art may be cinematic and ominous, but should fit the actual church-produced event rather than depicting an enormous fantasy fortress or a scale the live event cannot support.
- Visuals should generally support the seriousness of the message rather than rely on gore.
- For individual Facebook hype-ad prompts or image generation, first read `Advertising/Facebook hype ads.md` and apply its shared format, composition, style, tone, and scale rules in addition to the current room script and room-specific notes.

## Slides and presentation media

- Tour of Hell presentation slides have been produced in a **16:7.5** aspect ratio.
- Keep visual systems consistent within a presentation or scene package.
- Audio, video, and effects should be designed around reliable event-night operation, not just one-time demonstrations.
