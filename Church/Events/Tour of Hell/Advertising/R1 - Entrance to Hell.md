# R1 — Entrance to Hell

Read and follow the shared Tour of Hell Facebook advertising standard in:

`Church/Events/Tour of Hell/Advertising/Facebook hype ads.md`

Apply that file’s required format, composition, typography hierarchy, hand-drawn concept-art treatment, restrained theatrical scale, biblical tone, and `TOUROFHELL.CC` placement.

Then use the following room-specific direction for **Room 1 — Entrance to Hell**.

## Scene basis

Base the image on the current Room 1 script and Matthew 25:41.

The live scene presents a familiar fire-and-brimstone / worldly depiction of Hell: a dark passage, walls of fire, black rock-like textures, irregular pavers forming the safe path, orange light simulating lava, and the warning that the theatrical representation is only a picture while the biblical warning behind it is real.

For the advertising image, take limited artistic liberty by using a restrained pair of **gates of Hell** as the threshold at the end of the path. Keep the scale modest and consistent with the live church-produced scene.

## Upper-third text

Use:

**DO NOT FEAR MERE MORTALS;**  
**FEAR HIM WHO HAS POWER**  
**OVER THE SOUL**

## Central visual

Show a narrow path of **rough, irregular stepping stones** crossing a dark, enclosed passage.

Around the path, the floor should resemble **dark volcanic crust with intense orange-red heat glowing through cracks beneath it**. Suggest a lake of fire without making the lava look soupy, watery, or like a flowing river.

The path should visually lead toward a pair of **simple, partly open iron gates** in the middle distance.

The gates should feel like a **threshold into Hell**, not the entrance to a kingdom or fortress.

Behind the gates, show:

- restrained red-orange glow
- smoke
- heat haze
- darkness
- only a suggestion of fire beyond

Keep the space beyond unknowable and threatening rather than grand or expansive.

## Warning sign

Include an old, weathered wooden sign near the path or gate.

The sign must be **hanging by old rope** from a rough beam, hook, or simple support. It must **not** be mounted on a post.

It should read:

**ABANDON ALL HOPE**  
**YE WHO ENTER HERE**

Keep this sign clearly secondary to the upper-third text. It should feel like part of the environment, not another headline.

## Environment

Use room-specific elements such as:

- black volcanic or scorched rock textures
- dark enclosed walls
- drifting smoke
- subtle heat haze
- glowing cracks
- low orange-red illumination
- occasional small sparks or embers
- heavy shadow

The setting should feel **tight and enclosed**, loosely evoking a church-basement theatrical passage rather than a vast fantasy landscape.

The stepping-stone path should remain the clearest visual route through the scene.

## Biblical emphasis

The image should function as a visual warning about judgment and eternity, supporting the Room 1 script’s use of Matthew 25:41 and its warning of **“everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels.”**

The emphasis is on **crossing a threshold into danger and judgment**, not on depicting Satan’s domain.

The image is a theatrical symbol, not a claim about the literal appearance of Hell.

## Do not include

- enormous gates
- castles
- citadels
- palaces
- throne rooms
- demonic statues
- Satan
- demons standing guard
- banners
- armored guards
- armies
- people standing on walls
- elaborate fantasy architecture
- giant cliffs or landscapes that make the live room seem much larger than it is
- overly liquid or soupy lava
- gore

The finished image should communicate a **dangerous threshold**: a rough path across glowing heat and darkness toward restrained iron gates, with the hanging warning sign reinforcing the seriousness of what lies ahead.
