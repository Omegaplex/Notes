# R0 — Book of Life

Read and follow the shared Tour of Hell Facebook advertising standard in:

Church/Events/Tour of Hell/Advertising/Facebook hype ads.md

Apply that file’s required Facebook format, upper/middle/lower composition, typography hierarchy, hand-drawn concept-art treatment, restrained theatrical scale, biblical tone, and TOUROFHELL.CC placement.

Then use the following room-specific direction for Deck — Judgment.

SCENE BASIS

Base the image on the current Deck — Judgment script and its biblical references:

- Revelation 20:12–15
- Acts 17:30
- Romans 4:7
- Romans 6:23

The central ideas are:

- judgment according to one’s works
- sins left uncovered
- the opportunity to repent having passed
- the wages of sin being death
- a name not found written in the Book of Life

The scene should feel solemn, judicial, spiritually serious, and final.

UPPER THIRD TEXT

Use:

IS YOUR NAME WRITTEN
IN THE BOOK OF LIFE?

CENTRAL VISUAL

Show one ordinary young person standing before judgment.

The accused should wear contemporary everyday clothing and should not look like a criminal, villain, or obviously evil person. Their posture should communicate realization, apprehension, and finality rather than screaming or exaggerated terror.

In front of them is a modest judgment station with a large open book representing the Book of Life and several papers or records suggesting that their works have been examined.

The open book should be one of the strongest visual elements in the scene.

JUDGE

The Judge should not appear human, but should also not be demonic.

Portray the Judge as a solemn heavenly being carrying out divine judgment.

The Judge should feel:

- authoritative
- ancient
- holy
- otherworldly
- emotionally restrained
- awe-inspiring rather than frightening

Use subtle visual cues such as:

- pale or softly radiant presence
- restrained supernatural light
- grave, commanding posture
- flowing ceremonial robes
- subtle luminous eyes or facial illumination if visible

Do not portray the Judge as God the Father, Jesus, a demon, or a fantasy king.

The Judge may have one hand resting near the open Book of Life or the records, as though the evidence has just been reviewed.

BAILIFF

Place a Bailiff beside or slightly behind the accused.

The Bailiff should also be a non-human heavenly being, not an ordinary human and not demonic.

The Bailiff should feel:

- strong
- silent
- solemn
- immovable
- obedient to divine authority

Use restrained celestial or otherworldly qualities, but avoid fantasy armor, weapons, grotesque features, or exaggerated angel imagery.

The Bailiff’s presence should communicate that the verdict has been given and the accused is about to be taken away.

Do not depict violence or physical struggle.

SCRIPTURAL VISUAL THEMES

The scene should visually support the current script:

“You are judged by your actions and according to your works.”

“You had the opportunity to repent, but your sins remain uncovered.”

“The wages of sin is death.”

and especially:

“Your name is not found written in the Book of Life.”

Use the open book, records, Judge, accused, and Bailiff to communicate Revelation 20:12–15 without placing large amounts of extra readable text in the environment.

Do not show literal lists of sins.

WHAT COMES NEXT

Subtly suggest the direction the accused will be taken after judgment.

Off to one side or behind the Bailiff, show:

- a dark stairway heading downward
- the stairs descending toward a door or passage leading to Hell
- restrained red-orange illumination coming from below or beyond
- deep shadow past the doorway

The stairway must clearly be going down, not up.

This should foreshadow the Entrance to Hell without turning the judgment scene itself into a fire-and-brimstone image.

DO NOT INCLUDE

- human-looking Judge or Bailiff
- demons
- grotesque creatures
- fantasy warriors
- heavenly palace architecture
- giant celestial courtroom
- throne-room imagery
- clouds or pearly-gate imagery
- oversized scales of justice
- prison bars
- gore
- flames surrounding the judgment table
- large readable banners or extra slogans

The finished image should communicate the finality of judgment and the personal significance of the Book of Life, with an ordinary person standing before solemn heavenly beings carrying out divine judgment.
